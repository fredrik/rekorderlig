Someone invited you. First — what should we call you?

How it works

  • Thumb titles up or down. A card shows only what the model can see — the title and where it came from. No score, no points.
  • A small model learns the pattern. Twelve cards to a round; at the end of each one it retrains and tells you whether it actually got better.
  • Hacker News, reranked for you. The Feed tab sorts the whole corpus by what it has learned. It is yours alone.

Nothing you do here is undoable — change your mind in Votes, and the next round learns the new answer.
